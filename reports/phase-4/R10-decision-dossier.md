1. **Recommendation: don't spend big yet. Run a cheap demand test first (about CAD 2,500–4,500, 6–8 weeks). Then launch the powder within the CAD 15k budget, and add the can only once people are coming back to buy again and the money is in place.**

5. **Verdicts:** Budget plan = **GO WITH CONDITIONS**. Best plan = **GO WITH CONDITIONS, staged.** Sign nothing for the can until the powder passes its gates and the funding is approved.

### 2.3 First-run size and cost per can

**Published anchors:**
- Typical RTD coffee co-packer minimum: **15,000–30,000 cans**. Small roasters often start at about 2,000 (one pallet) [SRC-R10-01].
- Canned beverage on a craft/sleeved line: **2,500–10,000 cans**. Industrial printed cans: about 180,000 [SRC-R10-02].
- RTD coffee without protein: USD 1.85 per can at the start, down to 1.35 at volume, plus USD 0.30–0.40 per can shipping [SRC-R10-01]. Nations: USD 1.99 @2,400 / 1.49 @4,800 / 0.99 @9,600+ [SRC-R6-13].
- Eightx illustrative model: variable cost USD 1.10 per unit plus about USD 4,000 setup/changeover. That gives USD 0.80 per unit of fixed fee at 5,000 units [SRC-R10-02]. Simple shelf-stable setup is "near $2,000" [SRC-R10-02].
- Deposits are 30–50% at booking, and printed packaging is often 100% prepaid [SRC-R10-02].

**Formula (ESTIMATED):** CAD per can = (V + S ÷ N + P) × FX + sleeve + inbound freight
- V = USD 1.10 [SRC-R10-02 model]. S = USD 4,000 [SRC-R10-02 model]. N = cans in the run. FX = 1.4247 [SRC-R8-03].
- P = premium for plant protein, stabilisers and retort/aseptic: USD 0.40–0.80 (**ESTIMATED**, no public figure).
- Sleeve = CAD 0.15–0.30 (**ESTIMATED**). Freight = CAD 0.15–0.35 (**ESTIMATED**, anchored on USD 0.30–0.40 [SRC-R10-01]).

### 2.6 Alternative: a "Keep Refrigerated" small batch

- A low-acid food in a sealed container can skip commercial sterility if it is sold refrigerated with a "Keep Refrigerated" label [SRC-R4-05].
- **Niagara College** runs batches of 50–1,000 L and does canning and pasteurization, with CFIA licensing and approval for interprovincial trade [SRC-R10-05]. 1,000 L ≈ 3,000 × 330 mL cans (arithmetic). **Guelph** offers HTST/UHT and short commercialization runs [SRC-R10-04].
- **Why it fits:** events already need coolers. It allows a summer 2027 event pilot of about 2,000–3,000 cans without a 5k–10k retort commitment. Cost: UNKNOWN (fee-for-service), ESTIMATED CAD 8,000–15,000 including development.
- **Risks:** short shelf life (ESTIMATED 30–90 days), cold chain to every event, no ambient online shipping. Refrigerated low-acid protein drinks still carry a pathogen-control risk (*C. botulinum*, ESTIMATED), so a process authority must set the process. Whether a college pilot plant can make product for sale is **not verified**.

### 2.7 Main risks

1. **Demand and channel (high).** Only one sober dance date is confirmed in the next 90 days, in the evening [SRC-R9-03]. The Coffee Party shows 0 upcoming events [SRC-R9-07]. You have no audience. Winter cuts outdoor runs. Selling 5,000–10,000 cans before they expire depends on events that may not exist.
2. **Technical (high).** Plant protein in a sterilised can can bring sediment, chalkiness, off-notes and gelling [SRC-R4-06]. Nitro foam with protein is unproven (UNKNOWN). Retort formulation is "tedious and time-consuming" and few co-packers take new retort customers [SRC-R10-08]. Development takes 6–12 months [SRC-R4-06].
3. **Capital (high).** CAD 60k–135k peak against a CAD 10–15k budget. It would be largely debt-funded, with deposits of 30–50% and possibly 100% prepaid packaging [SRC-R10-02]. Unsold cans tie up cash.
4. **Copycat (medium).** The difference is brand and channel. The Coffee Party has its own coffee brand [SRC-R3-01], and Nabob has run its own coffee party [SRC-R3-11]. Brüst is scaling into the US [SRC-R0-18].
5. **Regulatory wording (medium).** "Energy" and "recovery" wording, the FOP sugar threshold, the dairy-free claim, alcohol adjacency at events (R5 §2.4, 2.7).

### 2.8 Kill criteria (best plan)

Stop the can track, keep only the powder, if **any** of these happens:
- Gate 1 fails any R8 test: pre-order <3%, failed blind taste test, <3/8 organizers say yes, or gate spend >CAD 7,000 [SRC-R8 §3.4, 3.6].
- Bench samples fail the can's own taste test: <60% rate 4–5/5, or <50% prefer it over Brüst or OWYN poured blind (R7 §3.4 method).
- Fewer than **2 co-packers** quote a run of ≤10,000 cans, or the landed cost is >CAD 4.00 per can at the chosen run.
- Funding approved is less than **peak cash + 20%**.
- Fewer than **12 confirmed event slots** for May–Sep 2027 by March 2027.
- Repeat purchase on the powder is <25% at the second gate (~day 150) [SRC-R8 §3.6].
- Stability: visible sediment that won't shake back in, or a sensory fail, at the 3-month real-time check.

## 5. GO / NO-GO

### Budget plan: **GO WITH CONDITIONS**

**Why go:**
- It is the only plan that fits the stated budget.
- It measures the thing that matters most, repeat purchase.
- The worst-case loss is capped at about CAD 7,000 at Gate 1 [SRC-R8 §3.6].

**Conditions:**
1. Run the Micro-test (3.3) first. If it fails, stop at about CAD 4,000 spent.
2. Apply the 3.2 cuts so the corrected base stays ≤CAD 13,000.
3. Counsel confirms in writing: food classification, sampling rules for imported stock, and the SFC position [SRC-R5 §2.12].
4. At least 3 organizers say yes. Plan the 6 pre-gate events around **weekly run clubs and DJ fitness classes**, because only one sober dance date is confirmed [SRC-R9 §3.2].
5. If quotes or week-8 data look like the cautious case (≈CAD 20,600 peak), it becomes **NO-GO**.

### Best plan: **GO WITH CONDITIONS, staged (not a GO today)**

**Why not today:**
- It needs about 5–10× the stated budget.
- There is no audience.
- The event calendar for the next 90 days is nearly empty.
- The plant-protein can is a 6–12-month technical project [SRC-R4-06, R10-08].

Committing now would put CAD 60k+ of mostly personal debt behind untested demand. **Why keep it alive:** the can is the format that suits the events best, and there is no small Canadian plant-protein coffee can in the market (section 6, proof note). **Conditions before signing any can contract:**
1. Gate 1 passes.
2. ≥2 co-packer quotes ≤CAD 4.00 per can at ≤10,000 cans.
3. Funding approved ≥ peak + 20%.
4. Bench samples pass a blind test.
5. ≥12 summer 2027 event slots are confirmed.

If those can't be met by about March 2027, run the refrigerated pilot (2.6) for summer 2027, or switch to the budget-plan trigger (3.4).

**If both plans fail their kill tests, stop. That is the overall NO-GO.**
