# R12: Toronto daytime and sober events, coffee + protein shelf benchmarks, small paid-social test benchmarks

*Desk research from public web pages only. Accessed 2026-10-20. Date information is "as of 2026-10-20". Every number has a source ID [SRC-R12-NN] or is labelled ESTIMATED or UNKNOWN.*

## In short

- **Where to show up:** Toronto has a real daytime "coffee party" scene. The biggest series, The Coffee Party, grew from about 250 to 1,000+ people per event in 2024-25 [SRC-R12-01][SRC-R12-03]. In 2026 there are smaller copies of the format, such as House Brew, Coffee Rave (house and techno) and Fresh Ravers [SRC-R12-06][SRC-R12-07][SRC-R12-08]. Run clubs are the steadiest weekly option. At least one Toronto run club (Dupont Run Club) already brings in drink brands for sampling: a yerba mate brand and an electrolyte brand [SRC-R12-14].
- **What to taste against:** Most coffee + protein drinks on Canadian shelves use milk protein: Premier Protein, Super Coffee, brüst and the Starbucks and Tim Hortons protein drinks. The only plant-protein coffee drink we found sold in Canada is OWYN, and it is imported [SRC-R12-26].
- **Ads:** Plan on roughly **CAD 4-40 per email sign-up**. A CAD 300-600 test should buy about **8-170 sign-ups**, with a base case of about **25-50** (ESTIMATED, worked out from the benchmarks in Part C).

**Takeaways for a small brand:**

- **Easiest first step:** sample after a run at weekly clubs that already let drink brands in. Dupont Run Club and the cafe-based clubs (Manic RC, Sunday Pace) are the best fits.
- **Best on-brand stage:** the techno/house "Coffee Rave" style parties (#3 and #4). They are small and new, and none lists sponsors yet. A small organiser is more likely to agree to an in-kind deal, such as free product in exchange for a table (ESTIMATED).
- **Winter caveat:** The Coffee Party used outdoor venues in spring and summer [SRC-R12-05][SRC-R12-10]. Between November and March, expect indoor cafe and bar venues with fewer people (ESTIMATED).

In plain words:
- **CPM** is the cost to show the ad 1,000 times.
- **CPC** is the cost per click.
- **Landing-page conversion** is the share of visitors who leave their email.

The US "cost per lead" figures come mostly from Meta's built-in forms, which convert better than sending people to your own page [SRC-R12-51]. That makes them optimistic for a test that sends people to a landing page.

### C2. What these mean for a Toronto test (converted to CAD)

| Metric | Range for your test (CAD) | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| CPM | **CAD 15-30** | Canada CPM of USD 11-17 [SRC-R12-46][SRC-R12-47] x 1.42 [SRC-R12-55] gives CAD 16-24. Narrow Toronto targeting and Nov-Dec holiday competition add more (ESTIMATED) |
| CPC (link click) | **CAD 0.70-2.00** | US traffic CPC of USD 0.45-0.80 [SRC-R12-49][SRC-R12-50] x 1.42 gives CAD 0.64-1.14. Raised at the top end for a brand-new account, a narrow audience and no proven ad creative (ESTIMATED) |
| Landing-page sign-up rate | **5-20%** | 4.8% paid-social median, 7.1% F&B median [SRC-R12-54]. A free-sample or event-invite offer could push the rate higher (ESTIMATED) |
| Cost per email sign-up | **about CAD 4-40; base case about CAD 12** | CPC divided by sign-up rate (ESTIMATED). For comparison: F&B lead-form cost of USD 3.16, about CAD 4.50 [SRC-R12-49]; all-industry USD 27.66, about CAD 39 [SRC-R12-49] |

### C3. How many sign-ups CAD 300-600 might buy (ESTIMATED scenarios)

| Scenario | CPC (CAD) | Sign-up rate | Cost per sign-up | CAD 300 buys | CAD 600 buys |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pessimistic | 2.00 | 5% | CAD 40 | about 150 clicks, **about 7 sign-ups** | about 300 clicks, **about 15 sign-ups** |
| Base | 1.20 | 10% | CAD 12 | about 250 clicks, **about 25 sign-ups** | about 500 clicks, **about 50 sign-ups** |
| Optimistic | 0.70 | 20% | CAD 3.50 | about 430 clicks, **about 85 sign-ups** | about 860 clicks, **about 170 sign-ups** |

Impressions: CAD 300-600 at a CPM of CAD 15-30 buys about **10,000-40,000 ad views** (ESTIMATED).

**Practical notes:**
- **TikTok's minimum budget eats most of the money.** TikTok requires more than $20 a day per ad group [SRC-R12-53], so a single TikTok ad group over 14 days costs at least $280. That is almost all of a CAD 300 budget. With CAD 300-600, put most or all of the money on Meta (Instagram Reels and Stories), or run TikTok alone (ESTIMATED judgment).
- **Food & beverage TikTok ads get few clicks.** Their benchmark link CTR is low (0.58%) [SRC-R12-52]. Expect cheap views but expensive clicks.
- **The sample will be small.** About 25-50 sign-ups is enough to tell "some interest" from "no interest". It is too few to compare more than 2 ad versions reliably (ESTIMATED).
