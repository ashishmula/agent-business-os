# DEC-0004: Positioning route is the daytime coffee-rave brand

- Date: 2026-10-18
- Decided by: Ashish (R3 "You choose" gate, decision card)
- Status: Accepted

## Decision
Research route A in depth: a techno/electronic-music coffee-protein brand sold at sober daytime events in Toronto (coffee raves, sober raves, run clubs and workouts with DJs). Protein type (whey or plant) stays open; it is not tied to the plant-based route.

## Why
- R3 scores: coffee rave 3.65, plant-based 3.60, decaf 3.20, real-coffee taste 2.95, older adults 2.65 (`reports/phase-4/R3-positioning-routes.md`).
- Comes with a crowd, which answers the "no existing audience" problem.

## Guardrails carried forward
- Never promote with alcohol or at alcohol-serving events (Health Canada: adding caffeine to alcoholic drinks is illegal).
- "Energy" or "recovery" wording on the pack needs counsel.
- Kill criteria from R3: under 3% of samplers pre-order, under 20-25% repeat, failed blind taste test, or a Canadian run above about CAD 8,000.
