# DEC-0003: First market is Canada, US second

- Date: 2026-10-18
- Decided by: Ashish (R0 "You choose" gate, decision card)
- Status: Accepted

## Decision
Launch first in Canada (GTA in person plus online across Canada). Every Phase 4 report also notes what would change for a US launch, the planned second step.

## Why
- R0 scores: Canada 3.90, US 2.75, UK 1.95, Australia 1.85, EU 1.80 (`reports/phase-4/R0-market-selection.md`).
- No customs; the only market Ashish can test cheaply in person.
- US is larger but the most crowded, and since 29 Aug 2025 small parcels from Canada need full customs clearance.

## Context recorded with the decision
- Ashish has no existing audience; routes must include winning first customers from zero.
- White label alone does not differentiate; R3 tests each route on "who buys it and why you over Brüst".
- Ashish proposed a techno/music-themed brand; included as an R3 route, steered away from alcohol-serving club nights.

## Revisit if
Canadian counsel finds the product needs a licence or category that makes a Canadian test much costlier than estimated.
