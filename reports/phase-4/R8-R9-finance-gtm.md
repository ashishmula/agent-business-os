## 1. Plain-language summary (5 lines)

1. **Base case: you need about CAD 12,900 of cash at the worst point (month 4).** That fits the CAD 15,000 cap but not the CAD 10,000 floor. You do not break even in 6 months: you end month 6 about CAD 7,800 down, with about 170 pouches still in stock, and break even around month 11–12 (all ESTIMATED).
2. **Price: CAD 44 for a 15-serving pouch (CAD 2.93 per serving).** That sits inside the Canadian powder band of CAD 2.70–3.60 per serving [SRC-R1 price bands]. Each pouch costs about CAD 14 to make and land, plus about CAD 4.50 in card fees and shipping (ESTIMATED; the factory price is a guess until we get quotes).
3. **Spend in two steps.** Step 1 (weeks 1–8): about CAD 5,200–7,900 on stock test pouches, a blind taste test and 6 sober daytime events with free samples and pre-orders. Step 2 (only if every kill test passes): label review, lab tests and one Canadian production run.
4. **Sell direct to people first (B2C), and add a small wholesale side (B2B) for run clubs and cafés.** Organizers and cafés are the way in to events, but they will not buy in volume from a brand nobody knows.
5. **Stop-loss rule:** stop at week 8 if any kill test fails or spend passes CAD 7,000. Never let total cash out go past CAD 13,000. In the cautious case even "just passing" the tests would need about CAD 19,000, so cautious-looking numbers at week 8 mean **no-go**.

## 3. R9: Go-to-market

### 3.1 Channel choice: B2C vs B2B vs hybrid

| Option | How it works | For | Against | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **B2C** (events + Shopify + Instagram/TikTok) | Sample at sober daytime events, take pre-orders by QR, ship or hand over at events | Highest margin (56% contribution, base); you see each buyer's repeat directly, which is what the kill tests need | Slow from zero audience; shipping cost per order | Core |
| **B2B wholesale** (cafés, run clubs, organizers buy pouches or serve cups) | Sell at ~60% of retail (CAD 26.40 base) | Bulk orders; borrowed audience | Thin margin after fees; cafés rarely stock an unknown brand; The Coffee Party has its own coffee brand [SRC-R3-01]; no repeat data per person | Add-on |
| **B2B event partner** (sponsor/vendor slot, free samples) | Pay a fee or give product for a booth slot | Fastest way to reach 1,000 samplers | Vendor fees UNKNOWN; 3-week application lead time [SRC-R8-10] | Entry tactic |

**Recommendation: hybrid, B2C-led.** Use event partners only as the door in (sampling slots), sell B2C (pre-orders, Shopify, in person), and add a small wholesale line (base 20 pouches) for 1–2 run clubs or DJ-led fitness classes that want to resell or serve it. Do not chase cafés until there is repeat data. The reason is that the kill tests (pre-order %, repeat %) can only be measured on B2C buyers.

### 3.6 Stop-loss rule

1. **Gate stop-loss (week 8): CAD 7,000 gross.** If spend reaches CAD 7,000 before the tests are complete, or any kill test in 3.4 fails, stop. Refund pre-orders and do not order the Canadian run. Worst-case loss is about CAD 7,000, so CAD 3,000–8,000 of the budget is kept.
2. **Programme stop-loss: CAD 13,000 cumulative net cash out.** Never exceed this (it keeps CAD 2,000 in reserve under the 15k cap). If the month-4 forecast shows a breach, cut paid social and events first, then stop.
3. **Second gate (~day 150): repeat.** If repeat is below 20% among the first 60 buyers, sell through the remaining stock, do not reorder, and stop.
4. If the quotes or week-8 numbers look like the **cautious** case (peak about CAD 19k), that is a no-go even if each threshold is met.

### 3.7 What changes for the US step

- **Border:** de minimis is suspended for Canadian shipments (since 29 Aug 2025), so every parcel needs formal clearance, and postal shipments pay tariffs [SRC-R0-15]. Expect a US 3PL with bulk entry instead of parcels from the GTA (ESTIMATED).
- **Rules:** FDA facility registration and prior notice; a US importer for FSVP; decide conventional food vs dietary supplement for a powder [SRC-R0-13, -14, -35]. US Nutrition Facts label (not bilingual).
- **Price band:** US powders sell at USD 1.83–3.25 per serving [SRC-R1 price bands], cheaper than Canada, against Chike, Naked and Javvy [SRC-R1-13, SRC-R2-06, -07].
- **Channel:** you can't attend US events from Toronto, so the US step is online only: DJ-community content and creators, and following The Coffee Party's US cities (NY, Miami) [SRC-R9-06]. Card fees on US cards are higher than domestic (rate not verified on the official page).
- **Trigger:** start only after Canada passes the repeat gate and a second run is funded from cash flow.
